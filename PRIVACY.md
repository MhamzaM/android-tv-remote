# Privacy Policy — TV Remote

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

TV Remote ("the app") is an open-source remote control app for Google TV / Android TV and Roku TV, developed by MhamzaM. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Summary
The app does **not** collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app does on your device
- **Finds TVs on your local network.** The app sends discovery messages on your Wi-Fi network (mDNS and SSDP) to find compatible TVs. This traffic stays on your local network.
- **Talks directly to your TV.** Button presses, text you type with the app's keyboard, and app-launch commands are sent directly from your phone to your TV over your local network. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Remembers your TVs.** The names and local IP addresses of TVs you add are saved on your phone so you don't have to add them again.
- **Pairing certificate.** To pair with a Google TV / Android TV, the app creates a security certificate that is stored only on your phone and is shared only with the TVs you pair with.

All of this data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Permissions
- **Internet / network access** – to communicate with TVs on your local network.
- **Wi-Fi state and multicast** – to discover TVs on your Wi-Fi network.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
