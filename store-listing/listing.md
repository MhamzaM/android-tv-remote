## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Lost the remote, or tired of hunting for it? TV Remote turns your phone into a fast, simple remote control for smart TVs running Google TV, Android TV or Roku TV, including TCL, Hisense, Sony, Philips and many more.

FEATURES
• Finds TVs on your Wi-Fi automatically, or add one by IP address
• One-time pairing with the code shown on your TV (Google TV / Android TV)
• Power, arrow pad and OK, Back, Home and Menu
• Volume and channel buttons that repeat when held, plus Mute
• Use your phone's volume buttons to change the TV volume
• Play/pause, rewind and fast-forward
• Number pad and direct input switching (TV, HDMI 1–4, AV)
• Launch apps like YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video
• Type on the TV with your phone keyboard: much faster searching
• Remembers your TVs and reconnects automatically
• Dark design with haptic feedback

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. The app talks directly to your TV over your home network and sends nothing anywhere else.

OPEN SOURCE
The full source code is available on GitHub: https://github.com/MhamzaM/android-tv-remote

SETUP TIPS
• Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
• Roku TV: on the TV open Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps and allow network access.
• Turning the TV on from standby needs the TV's network standby / quick start option enabled.
